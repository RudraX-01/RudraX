# Primary Research References

1. **The Dragon Hatchling: The Missing Link between the Transformer and Models of the Brain**
   *Authors:* Adrian Kosowski, Przemysław Uznański, Jan Chorowski, Zuzanna Stamirowska, Michał Bartoszkiewicz
   *Year:* 2025
   *Venue/Identifier:* arXiv:2509.26507
   *URL:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26507
   *Used for:* Formalizing the BDH module, specifically its use of synaptic plasticity, scale-free network topologies, and the distinction between outer-product matrices and evolving network state.
   *Claim supported:* BDH working memory involves synaptic plasticity and concept-related specific responses.

2. **BDH-CQ: In-Context Learning with Recurrent Latent Reasoning**
   *Authors:* Björn Engdahl, Adrian Kosowski, Jan Chorowski, Zuzanna Stamirowska, Przemysław Uznański, Junlin Jiang, Rohan Phadke, Remigiusz Kinas, Richard Zhong
   *Year:* 2026
   *Date:* August 10, 2026
   *Venue/Identifier:* arXiv:2608.09888v1
   *URL:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09888
   *Used for:* Mathematical formulation of recurrent contextual memory ($S_t$) and iterative latent computation ($H_r$).
   *Claim supported:* Demonstrations modify recurrent memory, but compositional tasks like color-swap with relocation (0/72) still pose a specific object-property binding challenge.

3. **Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces**
   *Authors:* Albert Gu, Tri Dao
   *Year:* 2023
   *Venue/Identifier:* arXiv:2312.00752
   *URL:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00752
   *Used for:* Discussing limitations of earlier SSMs and selective state spaces' role in content-based reasoning.
   *Claim supported:* Broader discussion of memory/computation trade-offs.

4. **RWKV: Reinventing RNNs for the Transformer Era**
   *Authors:* Bo Peng, et al.
   *Year:* 2023
   *Venue/Identifier:* arXiv:2305.13048
   *URL:* https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13048
   *Used for:* The mathematical foundation of modern linear recurrent models that replace the KV cache.
   *Claim supported:* Fixed recurrent state does not grow with sequence length.
